Grux CLI onboarding, interactive prototype

Every feature, capability and permission below is read from the app's own Swift sources by extract-registry.py. The arithmetic on the COST screen is the app's arithmetic, not an illustration of it.

grux setup
Prototype only. Nothing here talks to a real Grux install. It exists so the flow can be driven, reviewed and asserted before any of it is written in Swift. Drive it from a console with __grux.send('down'), read it with __grux.state(), and check the arithmetic with __grux.derived().
